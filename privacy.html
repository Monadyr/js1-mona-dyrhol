Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At Square Eyes, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you visit our website, create an account or purchase films through Square Eyes.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Square Eyes, we may collect the following information:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address.
  • Account login information.
  • Billing and transaction information.
  • Films and products you purchase.
  • Information you provide when contacting us.
  • Technical information about your device and browser.
  • IP address and general usage information.

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary for providing and improving our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Square Eyes account.
  • Process and manage purchases.
  • Provide access to purchased films.
  • Communicate with you about your orders.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Maintain and improve our website.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Payments

Payments made through Square Eyes may be processed by a third-party payment provider.

Depending on the payment provider used, your payment information may be processed directly by that provider. Square Eyes does not necessarily store your complete payment-card information.

Payment providers may process your information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Square Eyes may use cookies and similar technologies to provide essential website functionality and improve your experience.

Cookies may be used to remember preferences, maintain sessions, understand how the website is used and improve our services.

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers to help operate Square Eyes.

These providers may include services for payment processing, hosting, analytics, authentication, email delivery and customer support.

Third-party providers will only receive information necessary to provide their services or where otherwise permitted by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing our services, maintaining records and complying with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, it will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request restriction of certain processing.
  • Request a copy of certain information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the information provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Square Eyes is not intended for users who are not legally permitted to use the service.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

11. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers may process personal information outside your country or the European Economic Area.

Where applicable, we will use appropriate safeguards required by applicable data-protection laws for international transfers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available on the Square Eyes website.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we process your personal information, please contact us.
